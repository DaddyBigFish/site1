About

Secured By Offense

An independent consultancy based in the United Kingdom. We are instructed for out-of-hours penetration testing, internal assessments, attack path simulation, and Cyber Essentials preparation, for private firms and public bodies.

Testing is not done in office hours. The window is from 18:00 UK time on weekdays, through the whole weekend, until 02:00 on Monday. A day is eight hours. Your staff keep the working day; we take the evening and the weekend.

The work is manual. Reports carry risk ratings and practical remediation. Where it applies we follow NCSC guidance. Nothing starts without written authority under the Computer Misuse Act 1990, a contract, signed Rules of Engagement, and a non-disclosure agreement.

Services

The work

Four lines of work, all delivered out of hours: web and API testing, internal infrastructure, attack path simulation, and Cyber Essentials.

01

Web application and API

Hands-on testing of web applications and APIs, against OWASP. Broken authentication, injection, business logic and access control — the things scanners miss. You get a technical report, risk ratings, and an order of work to remediate.

02

Internal infrastructure

A review of services and software on a defined network range. Domain credentials are optional; the work can be authenticated or not. Misconfiguration, exposed services and known weaknesses, written up with a risk rating.

03

Attack path simulation

A wider piece of work, with the estate in scope. From supplied credentials or a corporate device we follow viable paths through on-premises and cloud — Azure, AWS and GCP — to a stated objective, usually administrative control. Each path is documented.

04

Cyber Essentials

Preparation for Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus. The scheme is NCSC-backed and often required for public sector and supply-chain work. A gap assessment against the five technical controls, advice on remediation, and a review of the submission before it goes in.
